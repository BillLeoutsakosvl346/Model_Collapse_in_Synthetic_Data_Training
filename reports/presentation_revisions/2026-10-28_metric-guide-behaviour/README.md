# Behavioural intuitions and curve interpretations

Text revision of the existing 11-page Google Docs metric guide, 28 October 2026.
Every metric family now explains the model's behaviour in everyday language,
then discusses the likely causes of its observed trajectory. Numerical examples
remain separate from behavioural intuition. Exact transient dips and peaks are
explicitly unresolved; proposed mechanisms are not presented as demonstrated
causes.

The revision adds two primary background references and one concrete observation
from the saved continued-cycle-1 history: decreasing synthetic training loss and
increasing real validation loss over epochs 1-5. It performs no new experiments.

`guide.json` contains the complete revised authoring plan. Its `figure_root`
points to the preserved original guide's 11 figures and equations in
`../2026-10-28_metric-guide/`; they were not regenerated or changed. All source
experiment reports, measurements, earlier authoring plans and delivery receipts
remain unchanged. `google_doc_delivery.json` records native and PDF verification.
